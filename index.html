Bhabhi
the art of shedding cards
by Usama Khalid
How to play?
Goal: Be the first to get rid of all cards.
Start: Whoever has the Ace of Spades plays it first.
Thoku: If you can't follow suit, play any card. The current lead-holder picks up the whole pile and leads next.
Clean trick: All follow suit → highest card wins, leads next. No pickup.
Bhabhi: Last player holding cards loses! 😄

Rang
match, stack, and dominate
by Usama Khalid
How to play?
Goal: Empty your hand. The last player still holding cards loses!
Play: Match the top discard by same suit or same rank.
Draw: No valid card? Draw 1 from the pile — turn passes.
2 of any suit: Next player must draw 2. Stack another 2 → 4 cards. Then 6, then 8…
10 of any suit: Next player's turn is skipped.
Joker (4 — one per suit): Play anytime. Cancels any pending draw-stack and you choose the next required suit!
